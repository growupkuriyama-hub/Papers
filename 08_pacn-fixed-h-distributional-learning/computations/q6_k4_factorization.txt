Exhaustive q=6 / d=3 direct-factor search
Date: 2026-10-04

Question
--------
Does an abelian group G of order 36 contain four 6-element subsets
B0,B1,B2,B3 such that every pair Bi+Bj is an exact factorization of G?

Normalization
-------------
Each Bi may be translated independently, so it is enough to enumerate
6-subsets containing 0. There are C(35,5)=324632 normalized candidates
per group.

Criterion
---------
For |A|=|B|=6 and |G|=36,

    G=A+B is an exact factorization

iff

    (A-A) intersect (B-B) = {0}.

Therefore a K4 witness is exactly four nonzero difference masks that are
pairwise disjoint.

Conclusion
----------
No abelian-group (4,2;6) direct-factor system exists in any abelian group
of order 36, even when the four factors are arbitrary subsets rather than
subgroups.

Scope
-----
This closes only the abelian direct-factor construction route for q=6.
It does NOT prove that VC_4=6 is impossible for every 4-ary incidence class
with observer budget t_h=36: a different grammar/shattering construction
could in principle avoid the direct-factor-system template.

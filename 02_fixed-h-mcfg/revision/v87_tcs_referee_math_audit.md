## 2. Mathematical attack findings

### Semilattice-indexed abelian-coset main theorem
Provisional result: no counterexample discovered in this source-level review; independent third-party mathematical verification remains necessary.

1. Fixed inputs in the global bound: finite alphabet Sigma, finite typing h, finite join-semilattice Y and eta refined by h, fan-out f, rank r. The auxiliary abelian group, morphism phi, subgroup H, coset map gamma and accepting h-types vary universally. Constraining eta via h ensures equal typed tuples have equal eta values. Joint equality of h and phi mod H saturates target membership.
2. Rectangle rigidity: reduction gives each nonterminal a productive sentence context accepting all of its yields. For that fixed context, phi modulo H depends only on the semilattice yield value. Equality of joined values after varying one child, with fixed siblings, cancels all other abelian contributions by linearity and nondeletion.
3. Height descent: at a node with two multivalued child OCCURRENCES, if both distinguishable values of the second were below a maximal first-child join value, rectangle rigidity would force their quotient values equal. Thus some join strictly increases height. The distinction between symbol and occurrence is essential.
4. Short representatives: lexicographically minimal (word length, number of nodes) derivations have no repeated (nonterminal, quotient value) pair along retained paths. Each symbol has at most |Y| quotient values. Retained branching uses one strict height decrease; there are at most max(0,ht(Y)-1) branching nodes per path. Terminal and pruned child costs are charged along the at most r^b paths, giving Bsharp = r^b |Y| (||G||+r|V|t_G), using t_G <= theta_G.
5. Coset transfer: assumption (i) joint h/quotient membership saturation, (ii) constant quotient on each typed non-start symbol, and (iii) a generated short representative of each untyped quotient value are all required. Induction generates Parikh lattice defects bounded by T=(r+1)B+||G||. The fixed h-regular component fibers have fixed semilinear bases. With m=|Sigma| equations, n=O(T^m) variables and coefficient magnitudes a=O(T), Papadimitriou's nonnegative small-solution bound n(ma)^(2m+1) gives exponent 3m+1 for the representative. The sample-cardinality factor gives total exponent 3m+2.
6. The resulting short typed tuple may NOT be generated by the corresponding source nonterminal. The relaxed distributional-witness proposition is essential. The arbitrary finite-group presentation is not assumed computationally effective.

Outstanding third-party audit points: each good-grammar/nonempty-component use; the regular-fiber semilinearity constants; formal Papadimitriou hypotheses with signed defect columns; empty-word conventions; complete verification of the 3m+1 exponent. Not a certified mathematical proof.

### Local-group typing theorem
Provisional result: no concrete counterexample identified; the group shortening transfer remains the highest-priority independent proof risk.

1. The known semigroup equivalence LG/no-U1/minimal ideal is attributed to Almeida et al. The original source supplies an additional ordered sliding-factor group typing construction.
2. The sliding gamma-factor map is NOT in general a homomorphism directly from letters to a group; the full local-group typing is a finite-monoid morphism, and the block-decomposition lemma supplies fixed cross-boundary group factors.
3. The noncommutative interval, order homogenization, and heavy antichain lemmas remain in the compiled group appendix. The LG shortest-typed-yield lemma says their proof applies with window-preserving replacements and sliding gamma values. This requires an adversarial independent re-derivation for all interleaved MCFG components and short/long blocks, rather than trusting an informal verbatim transfer.
4. With fixed h, its window and group are fixed: bw=O(||G|| thetaBar), nLambda=O(||G||), betaLambda=O(||G||^2 thetaBar), sample count O(||G||), total length O(||G||^3 thetaBar). Main theorem is the CAPPED operator guarantee.
5. This typing-side condition is not necessary for the rank-one guarantee: the explicit h_star semilattice quotient lies outside LG.

Outstanding third-party audit points: the precise hypotheses of the simultaneous group-interval lemma under sliding factors; the finite-semigroup factorization into sliding windows; fixed-parameter uniformity and label/path pumping.

### Fan-out-two rank-one non-CFL separation
Provisional result: the four stages of the archived proof are internally consistent, with no counterexample identified.
1. Displayed rank-one MCFG derives each of the c and d branches.
2. Taking the c branch by regular intersection and marking one c by inverse homomorphism yields the three-equal-counter language, proving non-CFL.
3. The support language a*c+b* union a*d+b* is strictly 2-local and typed by first/last-symbol windows. For equal h_star-typed tuples with an accepting context, center-letter differences constrain the joint Parikh offset. A different context preserves its branch constraints; cross-branch switching forces zero offset.
4. Substitutability at arity one forces c and d to be interchangeable in any untyped baseline L0. It yields context equivalence between c²b and c²b² and then every a^m c^(m+1)b^(m+t). A regular filter DFA pumps b's in one accepted c-branch word, contradicting membership in L_star.

Check the source's nonempty separator convention in the arity-one argument independently before external submission.

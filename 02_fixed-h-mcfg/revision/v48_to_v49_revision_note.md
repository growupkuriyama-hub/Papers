# MCFG v48 -> v49 revision note

## Purpose

v49 is a citation-provenance precision pass prompted by pre-submission review.
It does not change the mathematical statement or proof of the
minimum-rank-two theorem.  It makes the provenance of the four-paired-block
SLTAG lower bound explicit and cites the original source directly.

## Main change: provenance of the rank lower bound

In the proof of Theorem `thm:genuine-ranktwo-witness`, the homomorphic image
`K_2` is now identified, up to renaming the common separator, with the
language `L_2` recorded by Kato, Seki, and Kasami (2004).

The revised text now separates two bibliographic roles:

- **Kato, Seki & Kasami (2004), Section 2.1, equation (*2)** is cited for the
  later RNA-grammar / MCFG presentation of `L_2` as a member of
  `CFL \ SL-TAL` and for their attribution of the underlying separation to
  Uemura et al.
- **Uemura, Hasegawa, Kobayashi & Yokomori (1999), Proposition 1** is cited as
  the original source proving that the corresponding four-paired-block
  context-free language is not generated by an SLTAG.

This removes the possible reading that Kato--Seki--Kasami (2004) first proved
the lower bound.

claim was changed.  This revision is attribution/citation precision only.

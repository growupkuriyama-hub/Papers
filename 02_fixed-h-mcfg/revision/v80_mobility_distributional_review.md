# MCFG #2 v80 integration audit — mobility and distributional bounds

Date: 2026-10-18. Candidate branch: \`mcfg2-v80-mobility-short-distribution\`.
Base: main v79 (includes rule-only effective witness bound). Source research:
Claude PR #46, branch \`mcfg2-v79-fanout2-lower-bound-search\`,

## Bilingual manuscript additions

- \`lem:mcfg-delete-type\` and \`prop:mcfg-rare-type-sample\`:
  removing all strings of a single finite type preserves \`C_mcf(f,h)\`;
  every characteristic sample must contain a witness of any realized type.
  General set-driven argument requires characteristic samples for **both**
  the full and type-deleted targets.
- \`lem:mcfg-power-transfer\`: an accepted sufficiently long power
  implies global two-component transfer between compatible typed tuples.
- \`lem:mcfg-local-shift\`: a five-point Ramsey monochromatic cut set
  produces a distributionally exchangeable two-component pair for every
  sufficiently long accepted word. Existence of shifts does **not**
  imply a shortening algorithm.
- \`thm:mcfg-all-typing-doubling-obstruction\`: the companion CFG
  doubling family is eventually **not** (2,h)-tuple-substitutable
  for **every** finite typing, strengthening the old fixed-window-only
  proposition. This is a nonmembership result, not a sample lower bound.
- \`prop:mcfg-relaxed-witnesses\`: for every typed nonterminal X,
  allow a shortest member of its **target-language distribution class**
  even if it is not grammar-generated. Combined with short **untyped**
  derivation contexts this yields
  \`|K| <= |V||M|^f + |P||M|^{fr}\`,
  \`||K||_+ <= (|V||M|^f + |P||M|^{fr})
                (1+theta_G+||G||+(r+1) kappa_G)\`.
  This is EXISTENTIAL. It does **not** inherit v79's rule-only
  elimination of canonical-symbol witnesses, which requires
  actual minimizing derivations.
- \`thm:mcfg-abelian-filter-data\`: a new original-thickness
  polynomial bound for
  \`h^{-1}(Acc) ∩ phi^{-1}(gamma+H)\`,
  when a reduced good bounded-rank MCFG presents this target.
  Auxiliary abelian group, homomorphism, and coset are not fixed:
  the constants depend on finite alphabet, fixed h,f,r alone.
  It does not claim effective construction for arbitrarily
  ineffectively presented auxiliary groups.

## Proof audit checklist

- Type deletion: closure of f-MCFL under regular intersection; the
  fibre restriction is itself (f,h)-tuple substitutable.
- Mobility: verify BOTH accepting placements equal the same positive
  word and the separator is nonempty; h-powers coincide after period.
- All-typing obstruction: the second block is in the second binary
  subword, separated from the first block by an 'a'; moving d opening
  brackets alters the position of the first 'a' in the only ceiling-free
  target word.
- Relaxed witnesses: ensure the external tuple's whole-target
  distributions agree with actual L_X tuples; choose untyped A context
  of fixed material <=theta_G; keep BOTH symbol and rule witnesses.
  Trace Link and composition-witness completeness, including rank-zero.
- Abelian case: fixed semilinear presentations of regular h-fibres
  give constant number/size of period vectors. The difference lattice
  Lambda_T is generated by small rule deltas; there are polynomially
  many (in T) lattice vectors in fixed alphabet dimension. The
  nonnegative system has FIXED number |Sigma| of equations and
  polynomial variables/coefficient bounds; Papadimitriou's original
  1981 JACM small-solution theorem gives polynomially bounded
  variables, independent of the abelian group.
- Original source checked: Christos H. Papadimitriou, "On the
  Complexity of Integer Programming", JACM 28(4):765--768 (1981),
  doi:10.1145/322276.322287.
  The author's theorem bounds each nonnegative solution component by
  N(m a)^(2m+1), with m fixed.
- Source originality: A/B/C/R/D were first proposed in Claude's
  research-only PR #46; adapted into present EN/JP manuscript here,
  with proof reorganizations and scope caveats.
- Remaining critical review: exhaustive human inspection of new
  semantic witness lemma and arbitrary-group quantifiers.
  Publication review is explicitly a separate step from this
  internal consistency and proof audit.

## Clear limitations

- Open Problem \`op:fanout-two\` remains unresolved.
- No universal polynomial bound for arbitrary h and arbitrary
  (2,h)-substitutable languages is claimed.
- Conjecture \`kappa_G <= poly(||G||theta_G)\` remains open and
  should be researched on a separate branch.
- Theorems do not imply that the syntactic LG algebraic
  classification extends to individual-typing necessity.

\begin{lemma}[Locally trivial--group typings admit window--group refinements]
\label{lem:mcfg-li-group-window}
Let $\Sigma$ be a nonempty finite alphabet and
$h:\Sigma^*\to M$ a finite-monoid morphism.  If
$h(\Sigma^+)\in\mathbf{LI}\vee\mathbf{Grp}$, then
there exist $k,\ell\ge0$, a finite group $A$, and a
morphism $g:\Sigma^*\to A$ such that
\(h\preceq (h_\Sigma^{k,\ell},g)
 \quad\text{on all of }\Sigma^*\).
If $h(\Sigma^+)\in\mathbf{LI}$, the group factor can
be taken trivial, so $h\preceq h_\Sigma^{k,\ell}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a finite locally trivial semigroup $S$, a finite group
$A$, a subsemigroup $T\subseteq S\times A$, and an onto
semigroup morphism $\pi:T\to h(\Sigma^+)$.  For each
$a\in\Sigma$ lift $h(a)$ to a pair $(s_a,g_a)\in T$.
Multiplying these pairs extends the lifts to a morphism
$\rho:\Sigma^+\to T$ with $\pi\rho=h|_{\Sigma^+}$.
Let $s:\Sigma^+\to S$ and $g:\Sigma^*\to A$ denote the two
coordinate morphisms, with $g(\eps)=1_A$.

The positive image $S_0=s(\Sigma^+)$ is locally trivial.
Put $q=|S_0|+1$.  The standard finite locally trivial
identities $S_0^q=S_0E(S_0)S_0$ and
$euf=ef$ for idempotents $e,f\in S_0$ and $u\in S_0$
\cite{Pin1986,kuriyamaCFG}
imply that $s(w)$ is determined by its length-$q$
prefix and suffix whenever $|w|\ge2q$:
writing $w=pxz$ with $|p|=|z|=q$,
choose $s(p)=aeb$ and $s(z)=cfd$; writing
$s^1(\eps)=1$ in the adjoined identity monoid, we obtain
\(s(pxz)=ae\,(b\,s^1(x)\,c)\,fd=aefd=s(pz)\).
The parenthesized factor belongs to $S_0$ even if $x=\eps$,
because $b,c\in S_0$.  Words shorter than $2q$
are recorded individually by $h_\Sigma^{q,q}$.
Therefore equality of $h_\Sigma^{q,q}$-values on
$\Sigma^+$ implies equality of $s$-values.  Equality of
both window and $g$ values then implies equality of
$\rho$ values and hence of $h$ values.

The window typing distinguishes $\eps$ from every nonempty
word, so the kernel inclusion extends to all of $\Sigma^*$.
Taking $k=\ell=q$ proves the first assertion.
If $h(\Sigma^+)$ itself is locally trivial, use
$S=h(\Sigma^+)$, its identity projection for $\pi$,
and the trivial group.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Algebraic sufficient conditions for polynomial data]
\label{cor:mcfg-li-group-polydata}
Fix a nonempty finite $\Sigma$, a fan-out cap $f\ge1$,
a rule-rank cap $r_{\max}\ge1$, and a finite typing
$h:\Sigma^*\to M$.
Let $G$ be any reduced good fan-out-$\le f$,
rank-$\le r_{\max}$ presentation of a target in $\Cmcf{f}{h}$,
and put $\bar\theta_G=\max\{1,\theta_G\}$.
The capped reconstruction operator has a characteristic sample
of cardinality $O_{f,r_{\max},h}(\|G\|)$ with:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 \|\Wit(\widetilde G_0)\|_+
 =O_{f,r_{\max},h,\Sigma}(\|G\|^2\bar\theta_G),
 &\text{if }h(\Sigma^+)\in\mathbf{LI},\\[2pt]
 \|\Wit(\widetilde G_0)\|_+
 =O_{f,r_{\max},h,\Sigma}(\|G\|^3\bar\theta_G),
 &\text{if }h(\Sigma^+)\in\mathbf{LI}\vee\mathbf{Grp}.
\end{array}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For $\mathbf{LI}$, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:mcfg-li-group-window}
with trivial group and Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary-polydata}.
For $\mathbf{LI}\vee\mathbf{Grp}$, use the finite window--group
refinement from that lemma and
Theorem~\ref{thm:window-group-polydata}.
The parameters of the refining morphisms depend only on the fixed
typing and alphabet, not on $G$.  The bound on cardinality
holds in both cases.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
These are \emph{sufficient} algebraic conditions, not a
characterization of all typings admitting polynomial
original-thickness data.  The lower-bound question beyond
fan-out one in Open Problem~\ref{op:fanout-two} remains open.
The empty-alphabet case has only the word $\eps$ and is trivial.
\end{remark}

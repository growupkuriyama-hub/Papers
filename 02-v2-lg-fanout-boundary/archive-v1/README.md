**2026-10-30 Quadratic data for the binary $U_1$ typing at fan-out one (from the DeepSeek search, sharpened and verified):** New Proposition `prop:binary-u1-quadratic` (end of `subsec:binary-u1`), proof in the new final appendix `app:binary-u1-quadratic`. The block-code language $L_{m,n}=\{\mathtt a^{2^m},\mathtt b\}^{2^n}$ is $(1,s_{U,2})$-substitutable; with $\mathtt a^{2^m}$ compressed by a doubling chain it has a presentation with $\|G\|=6m+6n+9$ and $\bar\theta_G=2^m+2^n-1$, while its unique pure word has length $2^{m+n}$. For $m=n$: $\|G\|=12n+9$, $\bar\theta_G=2^{n+1}-1$, every characteristic sample (any class-wide set-driven operator) exceeds $\bar\theta_G^2/4$. Hence for this typing at fan-out one no bound $p(\|G\|)\bar\theta_G$ holds (contrast: $O(\|G\|^2\bar\theta_G)$ for LG typings); polynomiality remains open. This refutes DeepSeek's Conjecture C ($\tau\le\|G\|\bar\theta_G$; it used the uncompressed block $\mathtt a^k$). Abstract, Introduction (c), `rem:fanout-one-data`(b), `rem:binary-u1-scope`(a), Conclusion and Tightness updated. DeepSeek report kept in `revision/deepseek_binary_u1_f1_search_2026-10-30.md` (its Theorem 2 has a wrong step for pure factors in a mixed context; correct argument: $\mathtt b\mathtt a^{p+i}$ and $\mathtt b\mathtt a^{p+j}$ share a context, forcing infinitely many words). Check: `revision/check_binary_u1_quadratic.py`. EN 50 / JP 54 pages; EN/JP synchronized (140 labels, checker baseline 32).

**2026-10-31 Research report kept (not in the manuscript):** `revision/deepseek_s2_typewise_search_2026-10-31.md` (DeepSeek, search for a counterexample to Problem M among path languages with type-dependent additive targets $h_1\ne h_\zeta$, Astra's design specification). No counterexample; instead a theorem excluding the whole design: if all pure words of $L$ have one additive value $h_1$ and all mixed words another value $h_\zeta\ne h_1$ ("two-fibre" languages, values in $\mathbb Z^d$), then every reduced good presentation of rank $\le r$ has $\mu_1(L)\le(2r+1)|V|\bar\theta_G$. Verified by Claude, proof correct: each nonterminal has at most two additive values, differing by $\Delta=h_1-h_\zeta$ (via the short context $E_B$); a rule has at most one two-valued child, and a one-valued parent has only one-valued children, so two-valued nodes of a shortest pure derivation form a chain from the root of length $\le|V|$; the one-valued children of chain nodes have only pure yields, of length $\le\theta$ (the report's $2\theta$ is not tight). The argument uses neither substitutability nor the fan-out bound, so it holds at every fan-out, and also at fan-out one. Consequence: a superpolynomial rare-type family at fan-out two must not be two-fibre (several additive values on the pure side, non-commutative quantities, or typed structure). The report cites `prop:binary-u1-quadratic` and `prop:u1-weight-all-fanout`, now parked in v2. The script `check_s2_typewise_search.py` stays in the author's local folder.

**2026-10-30 Linear factor at every fan-out (Astra report, verified):** New Proposition `prop:linear-factor-all-fanout` after `prop:linear-factor-sharp` (§4), proof in the new final appendix `app:linear-factor-fanout` (Lemma `lem:app-lf-path`). ## Proven upper bounds (fixed alphabet, fan-out, rank and typing)

For a reduced good MCFG $G$ with rule thickness $\bar\theta_G$:

- Fixed prefix–suffix windows, with or without finite-group values (including noncommutative groups): $O(\|G\|^2\bar\theta_G)$ total encoded characteristic-data length.
- Positive-image local-group typings: $O(\|G\|^2\bar\theta_G)$ (improved from $O(\|G\|^3\bar\theta_G)$ on 2026-10-30).
- Lower bound at every fan-out: $\Omega(\|G\|\bar\theta_G)$ under every fixed typing, for asymptotically smallest presentations (Props. `prop:linear-factor-sharp`, `prop:linear-factor-all-fanout`); exact order for the trivial typing.
- Binary typing $\mathtt a\mapsto1$, $\mathtt b\mapsto\zeta$ onto $U_1\notin\mathbf{LG}$ at fan-out two: $O(\|G\|^3\bar\theta_G^5)$ (Thm. `thm:binary-u1-polydata`, 2026-10-30).
- Rank-one MCFGs: $O(\|G\|^3)$ under any fixed typing (also $O(\|G_{\mathrm{in}}\|^3)$ for any standard rank-one input).
- Bounds for arbitrary standard input MCFGs via component-pattern thickness were parked on 2026-10-29 (see `parked/2026-10-29_ic-slim/`).

Qualitative learning and characteristic-set cardinality are attributed to Yoshinaka's prior work; the quantitative owner-wise shortening and presentation-sensitive encoded-length results are the paper's focus. This manuscript does not claim a fan-out-two characteristic-data lower bound. The companion CFG paper and the present MCFG paper employ different reconstruction operators.

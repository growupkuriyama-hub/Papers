# #2 v2 — Distributional Learning of Multiple Context-Free Languages under Fixed Finite-Semigroup Typing: Local Groups and the Length of Characteristic Data

**Author:** Takayuki Kuriyama
**Target journal:** Theoretical Computer Science (to be submitted after the decision on the companion TCS-D-26-00494)
**Status:** full English draft, core-only version (36 pages); external reviews (ChatGPT comparison, DeepSeek referee, ChatGPT re-reading of two proofs) and citation audit addressed; final English reading done; Japanese version pending

This folder is the restructured ("new-born") version of paper #2. The mathematics is taken from the frozen v1 in [`../02_fixed-h-mcfg/`](../02_fixed-h-mcfg/) and reorganized around one claim and three main theorems:

- **Theorem A** — local-group upper bound $O(\|G\|^2\bar\theta_G)$ at every fan-out; linear factor $\Omega(\|G\|\bar\theta_G)$ under every typing at every fan-out; $\Theta(\|G\|\bar\theta_G)$ for the trivial typing.
- **Theorem B** — the boundary at fan-out one: pseudovarieties ⇔ $\mathbf{LG}$; outside $\mathbf{LG}$ explicit targets need exponential data.
- **Theorem C** — fan-out two: the exponential fan-out-one family does not lift (it is not tuple-substitutable under any fixed typing). Whether $\mathbf{LG}$ remains the boundary at fan-out two is open.

## Major findings

**M1. Corollary cor:isolated-data does not meet the hypothesis of Corollary cor:master (lines 515–517, 916).**
- *The problem.* Cor. master assumes a bound on t_rep(H,s) for **every** reduced good rank-≤2 grammar H. Theorem thm:isolated-letter (lines 862–865) only covers H whose productions have the forms X→YZ, X→Y, X→x. Rank-two good grammars may also have productions such as X→aYb. So line 916 ("The second claim is Corollary master") applies Cor. master without its hypothesis being met.
- *Why it is harmless.* The proof of Cor. master (lines 526–532) only uses the hypothesis for G^+, and G^+ has exactly these three forms.
- *Fix.* Restrict the hypothesis of Cor. master to reduced good grammars with productions A→BC, A→B, A→a ("λ-free BUNF"). Prop. lg-short covers this class as well, so Thm. lg-sufficiency(iii) is unaffected.
- *Verdict:* GAP (fixable).

**M2. Theorem C(i) and the abstract claim two letters, but Prop. prop:linear-factor uses three (lines 82–83, 162–164, 957).**
- *The problem.* Theorem C(i) states the linear-factor lower bound "for every typing over an alphabet with at least two letters", and the abstract says "for every typing". Prop. linear-factor assumes three distinct letters a, c, d. Nothing in the body covers |Σ| = 2.
- *Fix, option 1.* State "at least three letters".
- *Fix, option 2 (evidence only).* Prove a binary variant. With d ↦ bb and c ↦ b, i.e. w_i = a^i bb a^{q−i} b^M a^{q−i} bb a^i, brute force found that every subset of L_{q,k} is Clark–Eyraud substitutable for all q ≤ 6, k ≤ min(q,3). I did not write a proof. Parts (ii) and (iii) would carry over with adjusted constants.
- *Verdict:* GAP (statement not supported as written).

## Item 1 — Section 3

**Lemma lem:lambda-elimination (lines 418–480).**
- (i): VALID. Note that a unit production A→B of G with a nonempty yield forces B ∈ V^+, so that case is covered too.
- (ii): VALID. The reachability argument is correct. Each A_i on the chain is in V^+ because A_i ⇒* xAy and A derives a nonempty word. An off-chain child C ∉ V^+ is productive, so L_G(C) = {λ}, and the new unit production applies. Sizes: a binary production of symbol size 5 gives at most 5+3+3 = 11 ≤ 15, so |P^+| ≤ 3|P| and ‖G^+‖ ≤ 3‖G‖ hold.
- (iii): VALID.
  - First bound: the spine has at most N nonterminal nodes, and the last production on it is terminal, so there are at most N−1 off-spine children. This gives (N−1)τ_G+1.
  - Rule thickness: the context bound (N−1)τ_G carries over to G^+ by the reachability argument. Off-path children with empty shortest yield are removed by the new unit productions. Off-path children with nonempty shortest yield are in V^+ and derive that same word in G^+ by (i). The result is 3(N−1)τ_G+2 ≤ 3N(τ_G+1).

**Lemma lem:typed-transfer (lines 482–498).** VALID. The second claim needs D_{L∖{λ}}(x) = D_L(x) for nonempty x. This holds because uxv is never empty, and the lemma's proof states it.

**Lemma lem:measures (lines 500–511).** VALID. The last sentence of the proof (line 510) is commentary; move it out of the proof.

**Lemma lem:homogeneity (lines 358–368).** VALID.

**Corollary cor:master (lines 513–533).** VALID WITH CLARIFICATIONS.

## Item 2 — Lemma lem:rep-witness (lines 380–416)

Verdict: **VALID WITH CLARIFICATIONS.** I checked it against the proofs of C1 Thm 5.2 (C1 lines 997–1041) and Lemma 6.2 (C1 lines 1198–1215).

1. **Definition of χ′ (Minor, but must be fixed).** Line 395 says χ′ is obtained "from the same shortest dependency path that defines χ(X)". In C1, χ(X) is defined by minimising |u_X|+|v_X| (C1 lines 951–954), not by a path. The path appears only inside the proof of Lemma 6.2. If one instead took the spine of a derivation that realises χ(X), the context bound would come out in terms of τ^typ, not τ^rep, because that spine need not be short.
   - *Fix:* define χ′(X) directly. Take a shortest path in the dependency graph of retained typed symbols from a typed copy of S₀ to X, and expand each off-path sibling Y by ρ(Y). Then |u′_X|+|v′_X| ≤ (N_t−1)τ^rep, |t′_R| ≤ 2τ^rep, and every witness has length ≤ (N_t+1)τ^rep, exactly as in Lemma 6.2. The size bound transfers.
2. **Are all witnesses in L?** Yes, but say why. Any actual yields y_Y of the off-path siblings and y_X of X give a word of L. Each y_Y ≡_L ω(Y) ≡_L ρ(Y) by homogeneity in G: both words lie in L_G(B) ∩ ŝ^{-1}(ν), and B is reachable. Empty-type siblings have yield λ = ρ. Since ≡_L is a congruence, u′_Xρ(X)v′_X and u′_X t′_R v′_X ∈ L. Line 401 cites Lemma homogeneity "applied to the typed refinement", but that lemma is stated for good grammars and G̃ is not good. Argue directly in G via Q2 instead.
3. **Rule (U).** It fires. ρ(X) and t′_R are nonempty, both have type μ (t′_R is the product of its entry types along a typed production), and both occur in the context χ′(X) inside K. If ρ(X) = t′_R, nothing is needed.
4. **Rule (B).** It works. The entries of t′_R are terminals or ρ(Y_i), and every concatenation of consecutive entries is a factor of u′_X t′_R v′_X. Since [[ρ(Y_i)]] = Ŷ_i, the induction applies. Unit rules X→Y give t′_R = ρ(Y) directly. Collisions ρ(X) = ρ(X′) only add derivations, which soundness absorbs.
5. **Start copies and empty types.** These are handled correctly. χ′((S₀)_μ) = (λ,λ), and ρ((S₀)_μ) ≡_L ω((S₀)_μ) ∈ L, so it lies in K, and the start-rule step of C1 (lines 1034–1040) goes through. Empty-type symbols have ρ = λ and are omitted exactly as in C1.
6. **The list of four uses of ω (lines 411–414).** It also needs "[[ω(X)]] is an observed factor", which the first witness family provides. That use holds for ρ as well.

## Item 4 — Section 4 and Appendix A

**Lemma lem:heavy (lines 589–607).** VALID.
- Grafting preserves the root type and removes nodes, so no (nonterminal, type) label repeats on a path, and every path has at most |V||S| nodes.
- The heavy nodes form an ancestor-closed set, so there are at most c·|V||S| of them.
- The letter count is (r+1)θ̄ per heavy node.

**Lemma lem:buffered (lines 1213–1235).** VALID. There are at most 2k neighbours on each side, because their start positions lie in a window of 2k. A greedy choice keeps at least m/(4k+1) intervals. Gaps of at least 2k make the k-enlargements disjoint, and the boundary hypothesis keeps them inside y.

**Lemma lem:group-interval (lines 1237–1253).** VALID. π_j = R_iS′S^{-1}O_i^{-1}, so π_i = π_j gives S′ = S, and m+1 > |𝔾| values give the collision.

**Lemma lem:blocks (lines 1102–1124).** VALID for long blocks, which is the only case used. A (k+1)-factor cannot span a block of length at least 2k+1. Factors of the "second kind" lie in the fixed words plus the k-windows. The number of them between consecutive runs is |x_i|+k−1+…, which does not depend on the block contents. So the z_i are independent of J. The sentence "a short block equals its replacement" is correct because ŝ_{k,k} records short words exactly.

**Lemma lem:rees and Theorem thm:lg-characterization (lines 1135–1211).** VALID. I checked the right-identity r_j, the action identity, the Λ-coordinate propagation using S^k ⊆ K, and the factor indexing t = k..m−1 ↔ F₁..F_{m−k}. I also checked all the equivalences.
- Minor: (iv) is stated "with k = |S|", but the direction (iv)⇒(i) is proved for arbitrary k, ν, and the introduction (lines 209–212) uses it with k = 1. State (iv) as "s is local-group-determined (for some k, 𝔾, ν); moreover k = |S| and a maximal subgroup of K suffice."

**Proposition prop:lg-short (lines 609–659).** VALID.
- The case split works. If y is short, |Q| ≤ 2k−1. Otherwise at most 2k nodes meet the marked letters. If |Q₀| > (4k+1)(|𝔾|−1), then |Q₁| ≥ |𝔾|, which is exactly what Lemma group-interval needs.
- The replacements x_v are nonempty because H is good. They give ŝ_{k,k}(𝔟′) = ŝ_{k,k}(𝔟) for each block.
- The resulting λ(y_J) = λ(y) and |y_J| < |y| contradict s-minimality.
- The constant C_k = 2k+(4k+1)(|𝔾|−1) is correct. Using |𝔾| ≤ k, C_k·k ≤ (4k+1)k², which gives the final estimate.

**Theorem thm:lg-sufficiency (lines 661–687).** VALID.
- (i): With r = 2, 3(4k+1)k² = c_S/3.
- (ii): τ^typ ≤ (c_S/3)·N·3N(τ_G+1) = c_S N²(τ_G+1), with |V^+| ≤ N.

**Theorem thm:pseudovariety-boundary (lines 693–709).** VALID.

## Item 5 — Corollary cor:isolated-data (lines 880–917)

Verdict: **VALID WITH CLARIFICATIONS**, plus M1.

- **Transfer through G^+.** Correct.
  - G^+ has the forms Theorem isolated-letter requires.
  - L(G^+) = L∖{λ} ∈ C_s, with the same distributions.
  - k_V ≤ N and θ̄_{G^+} ≤ 3N(τ_G+1), which gives 3^8 C₀ N^30 (τ_G+1)^8.
  - Lemma typed-transfer then converts the bound on t_rep(G^+) into a bound on τ^rep_s(G), and Lemma rep-witness turns that into data.
  - Minor: the appendix setting (line 1508) asks for L(G′) = L with λ ∉ L. Apply it to L∖{λ}, not to L(G), and say so.
- **Case (ii).** Correct. Type 1 is realized only by pure words (the isolation property I1), and the theorem covers it. Type ζ is covered by Lemma app-h-other(i), whose spine argument is correct.
- **Case (i): is C_s ⊆ C_c correct?** Yes. In a semilattice s(w) depends only on c(w), so c(x) = c(y) implies s(x) = s(y). Then substitutability with respect to s implies substitutability with respect to c. This matches the direction stated at line 250.
- **Case (i): is the inequality between representative lengths justified?** The inequality t_rep^s(G^+;A,μ) ≤ t_rep^c(G^+;A,t′) is true, but line 909's justification is not the one needed. "≡_L-equivalent words of one c-type have one s-type" is trivial and irrelevant. The correct argument:
  1. Let x be a c-representative: c(x) = t′ and x ≡_L y for a yield y of A of c-type t′ and s-type μ.
  2. Then s(x) = s(y) = μ, because c is finer than s.
  3. By s-homogeneity (L ∈ C_s), y ≡_L every yield of A of s-type μ.
  4. Hence x is a candidate for the s-representative, and the inequality follows.
  Here t_rep^c is well defined because L ∈ C_c. Theorem isolated-letter is applied with the typing c, once for a and once for b. Lemma app-h-other(ii) covers content Σ.
- **"The grammar" paragraph (lines 1507–1514).** It matches the use: reduced, good, forms X→YZ | Y | x, and G^+ is the intended instance. The bounds "yield ≤ θ" and "context ≤ θ" are correct: take a shortest sentence through a production with that nonterminal on the left.

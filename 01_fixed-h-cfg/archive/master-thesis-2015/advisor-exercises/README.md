# Master's-period advisor exercises — historical archive

This directory preserves two problem sheets supplied by the author as historical material from his master's-period research. According to the author's provenance note, these were custom exercises assigned by Prof. Makoto Kanazawa during the development of the ideas that later became the 2015 master's thesis and, much later, the fixed-h CFG line of work.

## Files

- `monoid-partition.pdf` — one-page exercise sheet dated **June 5, 2014**. It asks for a single finite monoid/homomorphism simultaneously separating differences of two regular languages, and then pairwise disjoint regular languages by pairwise disjoint accepting subsets.
- `R-substitutable.pdf` — one-page problem sheet on relation-based substitutability. It defines `~`-substitutability and asks, among other things, to:
  - recover k,l-substitutability via an equivalence relation;
  - adapt Yoshinaka's learning algorithm;
  - prove a finite-index/regular-class CFG typing normal form;
  - compare Clark-Eyraud's characteristic set CS(G) with Yoshinaka's K_G;
  - establish learnability for `~`-substitutable context-free languages.

The second sheet is especially close to the roadmap of the later master's thesis: recognizable/finite-index equivalence relations, monoid-based typing, smaller characteristic sets, and positive-data learnability all appear here already as explicit problems.
